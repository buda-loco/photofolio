Benjamin Arnedo

Based in [City], Benjamin Arnedo captures the intersection of light, motion, and stillness. With a background spanning editorial photography and narrative filmmaking, his work exists at the boundary between the decisive moment and the carefully composed frame.

His approach is rooted in observation — finding the image that already exists within a scene rather than constructing one. Whether on set or in the field, he brings the same quiet intensity to every project.
