Dickson Shop Photos

Commercial

2026

About A documentary series capturing the daily rhythms of independent traders at Dickson Shopping Centre.
Date February 2026
Place Canberra, Australia
Client Dickson Shopping Centre

More than just pretty photos

Despite the inherent disruption of a photographer moving through a working shop floor, every business owner at Dickson welcomed me with remarkable warmth — a generosity that occasionally extended to treats and genuine smiles I wasn't expecting. There's something quietly profound about being invited, however briefly, into the rhythm of someone's livelihood.

It also raises a question I find myself returning to: how much visual documentation do businesses like these actually have of themselves? Some operators understand instinctively that strong imagery is an asset — that it shapes how the world perceives them before a single word is spoken. For others, it remains an afterthought, easily deferred.

My hope is that work like this shifts that equation — that when people see what's possible, and receive something they're genuinely proud of, the value of that investment becomes self-evident.
